About Rani Lakshmibai

Rani Lakshmibai, also known as the Rani of Jhansi, was one of the most prominent figures of the Indian Rebellion of 1857.

She was born as Manikarnika Tambe in 1828 in Varanasi. She later married Maharaja Gangadhar Rao, the ruler of Jhansi, and became known as Lakshmibai.

She is remembered for her courage, leadership and determination to protect Jhansi. Her bravery made her an enduring symbol of resistance and patriotism.
